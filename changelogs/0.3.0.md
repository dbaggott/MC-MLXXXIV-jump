Answers the report in https://github.com/dbaggott/MC-MLXXXIV-jump/issues/9.

- **Renamed to Auto-jump** in game: the Mods list, the config screen, the **Toggle auto-jump** key and the action-bar messages. Your settings and key binding carry over. It is still separate from vanilla's **Auto-Jump** option under Controls, which hops you up one-block steps.
- **Jumps later, at the lip.** At an edge distance of 0 the jump now comes on the last tick you can still jump, one or two ticks later than before, so a sprint jump keeps its full takeoff distance, which is what head hitters and max-distance jumps need. At every distance, it still never lets you walk off.
- **Follows your momentum.** Sliding toward a ledge after letting go of the keys now jumps too.
- **Stairs and slabs are no longer ledges.** Walking down a staircase or a slab path no longer jumps at any edge distance, which was the misfiring at 1.00. A drop beyond them is measured along the steps.

The reset buttons on the two toggles in the config screen stay: Cloth Config gives no way to hide them.
