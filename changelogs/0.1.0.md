The first release, for Minecraft 26.2 with Fabric.

- **Edge jump** jumps for you as you walk toward a ledge, once you are within a set distance of it, and always before you would fall off. It ignores ledges behind a railing, glass pane or anything else taller than a step.
- Configure the edge distance, an optional random jitter on it, and whether it works while sneaking from the **Mods** screen (with Mod Menu) or `config/mcmlxxxiv_jump.json`.
- The **Toggle edge jump** key (unbound by default, under Controls → Movement) turns it on and off.

Edge jump is off until you turn it on. Requires Fabric API and Cloth Config.
